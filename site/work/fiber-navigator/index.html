Summary

A network visualization instrument that functions simultaneously as a home internet router and as a research device. By visualizing and recording network connections in real time, it seeks to render the normally invisible operations of internet infrastructure both perceptible and subject to scrutiny.

When connected to the internet over a network cable, the navigator creates its own WiFi Hotspot.

Phones, laptops, TVs and other wifi-enabled devices can connect to this network and browse the web.

Internet connections made by these devices are then geolocalized, by spinning the globe until the indicator arm points to the location of the server that answered the user's request. Simultaneously, the orange lines and points map the internet infrastructure: land fiber, interoceanic cables and internet exchange points of varying sizes.
